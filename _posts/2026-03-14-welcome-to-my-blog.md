The internet is full of information, but clear and thoughtful explanations are often hard to find.

This blog explores topics across technology, coding, global news, economics, and travel, with a focus on making complex ideas easier to understand.

Through simple and engaging essays, I aim to break down complicated concepts, analyze interesting trends, and share insights from around the world. It’s also my way of spending time on the internet a little more productively.
